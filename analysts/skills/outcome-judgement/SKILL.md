# Outcome Judgement (End Achieved)

## Objective
After an action, judge whether the operation's intended end was actually achieved — the effect (break / deny / manipulate) took hold, or the intelligence was genuinely collected — and decide what happens next.

## When to use
- An action has fired and you must call success or failure against the objective.
- The technique "worked" but it is unclear whether it produced the intended operational end.

## Method
- Restate the intended end in observable terms, then judge against that — not against "did the tool run".
- For an effect: confirm it took hold, holds under the target's response, and was not silently reverted or contained.
- For collection: confirm the data is genuine, complete enough, and not planted or partial (see deception-detection).
- Weigh cost paid: what the action cost in exposure, access, or attribution, and whether that changes the win.
- Classify: end achieved / partial / failed / unknown-pending-confirmation, and derive the next move — exploit, re-attempt, or withdraw.

## Signals / outputs
- A verdict on whether the operational end was met, with the confirming observation.
- The residual cost/exposure the action incurred.
- A next-move recommendation: exploit, re-attack, collect more, or disengage.
