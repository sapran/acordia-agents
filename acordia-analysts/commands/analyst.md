# Short alias for `cyber-analyst`; that wrapper is the canonical one.

Hand the work below to the `cyber-analyst` agent (ACORDIA Analysis), which orchestrates this pillar.

It holds the operating picture, decides method, timing and risk, and runs the end-neutral loop: did we achieve the effect, collect the intel, or hold the access, and what now. It directs the four analysis legs and fuses their reads itself, and its product goes to a human operator who acts on it.

**Brief:** $ARGUMENTS

Dispatch it as a subagent if this harness allows dispatching a primary agent; otherwise switch the session to `cyber-analyst` and carry the brief across. If the brief is empty, ask what the operation needs before starting — do not invent scope.
