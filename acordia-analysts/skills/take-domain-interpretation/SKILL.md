# Domain & Language Interpretation of the Take

## Objective

Turn possession of material into understanding of it. Access is not comprehension: a complete,
authentic, well-parsed take in a domain nobody on the operation reads is worth approximately nothing,
and worse than nothing if it is read confidently and wrongly.

## When to use

- When the take is in a language the operation does not command, or a subject with its own
  vocabulary, conventions and tells — banking, insurance, energy dispatch, procurement, medicine,
  law, engineering change control.
- Before a domain claim reaches the operating picture or a briefing.
- When a document's significance depends on what is *conventional* in its field, which is invisible
  to a reader outside it.

## Method

- Separate the three failure modes before starting, because they need different remedies: you cannot
  **decode** it (format, encoding, script), you cannot **read** it (language), or you can read every
  word and still not know what it **means** (domain convention).
- Establish the document's genre in its own field first — is this a draft, a filed return, an
  internal memo, a template, a system-generated report? Genre governs how much weight a statement
  carries, and it is the first thing a domain reader sees and an outsider misses.
- Read the **conventions**, not just the content: what a field normally contains, what a normal value
  looks like, what is boilerplate, and therefore what is anomalous. An unremarkable number is
  evidence only against a baseline of the usual.
- Translate for **meaning and register**, not word-for-word, and preserve terms of art untranslated
  with a gloss where the term is the evidence. Record the original alongside the rendering so a later
  reader can challenge it.
- State confidence **separately for the reading and for the significance**: "I am sure it says X" and
  "I am sure X matters" are different claims and are frequently not both true.
- Name what remains unread. An honest inventory of the untranslated and the not-understood is a
  finding; silent omission turns a gap into an implied absence of evidence.
- Where the domain read changes what the material is worth, hand that back — it belongs in the
  take-value judgement and may change whether the source is worth its risk at all.
- Degradation: with no competent reader available, extract the structural skeleton — headings,
  parties, dates, amounts, identifiers, references to other documents — mark the body unread, and say
  so plainly rather than paraphrasing from a machine rendering as though it were understood.

## Signals / outputs

- What the material says, in the operation's language, with terms of art preserved and glossed.
- Genre and convention notes that make an ordinary-looking value legible as ordinary or anomalous.
- Separate confidence on the reading and on the significance.
- An explicit list of what was not read or not understood, and what would be needed to close it.
