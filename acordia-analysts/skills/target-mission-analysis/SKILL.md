# Target Business/Mission Analysis

## Objective

Produce a ranked model of the target's crown jewels and mission threads: what the organization is FOR, the end-to-end processes that deliver it, and the assets/dependencies those processes cannot function without.

## When to use

- Early in an operation to convert a broad target into a short list of high-value objectives.
- When an access decision needs a "so what" — is this system a means to mission effect or just noise.

## Method

- State the mission in one line: what value the target produces and for whom (revenue line, service, command function, supply obligation).
- Trace the mission thread end-to-end — the process, systems, data, people, and third parties that must all work for output to happen.
- Identify choke points and single points of dependence: the system whose loss stops or corrupts the thread.
- Map crown jewels: the data, keys, code, or relationships whose compromise yields disproportionate leverage.
- Rank objectives by mission impact x accessibility, not by technical shininess.

## Signals / outputs

- Crown-jewel register tied to concrete systems/data stores and owners.
- Mission-thread diagram with named choke points and dependencies.
- Prioritized objective list mapping "hit/hold this" to "this mission effect."
