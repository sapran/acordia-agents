# Deception Detection / Anti-Manipulation

## Objective

Judge whether the intelligence you are acting on has been planted, denied, or shaped to manipulate your decision, so the operation is not steered into a honeypot, a burn, or a wasted effect.

## When to use

- Evidence is unusually convenient, one-sided, or perfectly confirms your working hypothesis.
- The target has motive and capability to feed you a false picture (mature defender, deception program, prior contact).

## Method

- Assume adversary agency: ask what the defender would want you to believe and whether the current picture matches it too well.
- Separate what you observed directly from what was made observable to you; weight channels the target does not know you hold.
- Check for denial (things that should be visible and aren't) and deception (things visible that shouldn't be) as paired tells.
- Look for manipulation of the analyst: urgency, flattery of your hypothesis, single-source pivots, bait that rewards a specific action.
- Seek confirmation through an independent access path the target cannot see or shape; withhold commitment until it corroborates.

## Signals / outputs

- A judgement: picture is trustworthy / possibly shaped / likely planted.
- The specific tells and the channels most and least susceptible to manipulation.
- A recommendation to corroborate, probe, or back off — and what not to act on yet.
