## Why

Finished analyst reports can currently be technically sound yet force a human reader to reconstruct abbreviations, comparisons, and the evidence behind a claim. The shared reporting method needs an explicit reader-facing contract, and the specialist credential layout must implement it without weakening disclosure or no-target-contact boundaries.

## What Changes

- State four repository-directed reporting practices in the competency-map doctrine: expand terms on first use, retain a finished-report acronym chapter, use tables for genuine comparison, and place usable evidence references beside evidence-backed claims.
- Add the same compact contract and product-level reader check to `briefing-reporting`, preserving its existing reporting, rendering, working-directory, identifier, and credential protections.
- Integrate the acronym chapter, local source locators, claim-local references, and safe Aleph-link validation into the credential-sweep HTML layout.
- Add the observable human-readable reporting contract to `skill-library` without replacing its existing rendering and citation requirement.
- Bump the plugin version and regenerate the shipped ACORDIA map from the final authored tree.
