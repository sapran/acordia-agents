## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Selected analytic-spine skills preserve model, automation, and outcome boundaries

`naming-the-gaps` SHALL frame a gap as an explicit unknown in a target model whose closure changes a decision, while retaining its collection-route and ownership procedure as grid-derived detail. `human-automation-teaming` SHALL distinguish automated hypotheses from supported analyst conclusions and require retained evidence, confidence, provenance, and human override for a consequential judgement. `outcome-judgement` SHALL treat access held for later use as a possible operational end and distinguish evidence of operational performance from evidence of strategic outcome.

#### Scenario: Gaps expose the model boundary
- **WHEN** `naming-the-gaps` is applied
- **THEN** it produces decision-relevant questions about explicitly observed, inferred, or unknown target-model elements rather than a general request for more data

#### Scenario: Automation output remains revisable
- **WHEN** automation triages, scores, correlates, or proposes a conclusion
- **THEN** `human-automation-teaming` requires the output to be presented as a hypothesis with retained evidence, confidence and provenance until an analyst adopts or rejects it

#### Scenario: Access is not mistaken for strategic success
- **WHEN** `outcome-judgement` assesses an operation whose end is access held for later use
- **THEN** it judges whether the access is established, durable and quiet, and does not claim strategic success from access or collection alone
