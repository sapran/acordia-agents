# Gain/Loss Calculus & Re-Planning

## Objective
Weigh an option's expected gain against its losses — exposure, burned access, attribution, opportunity cost — and turn that balance into a concrete adjustment to the plan rather than a gut call to press on.

## When to use
- Deciding whether to escalate, persist, or stop after a partial result or a scare.
- The obvious next step buys progress but spends something scarce (access, stealth, tooling).

## Method
- Name the gain concretely: what the move advances toward the objective, and how much closer it gets you.
- Name the losses fully — not just failure, but exposure, burned capability, attribution, and the moves it forecloses.
- Account for the sunk cost you must ignore and the opportunity cost of the paths this choice closes.
- Compare against doing nothing / waiting; a positive move must beat the null option, not just look active.
- Feed the verdict back into the plan: proceed, scale down, switch method, or re-scope the objective.

## Signals / outputs
- A gain-vs-loss balance for the option, including what it forecloses.
- A go / scale / switch / stop recommendation with its threshold.
- The specific re-plan edit the calculus drives.
